Why `LLEventPost` is not declared (plan 728): the temporary of `thisBits & 0xf` in `DoModCalc` is a reload register. `reload` takes the first usable register of `potential_reload_regs[]`; a hard register that appears explicitly in the RTL goes behind the unused ones (public GCC 2.7.2.3 `reload1.c:486, 3640-3711, 1840-1889`). A call to an `int` function (or an undeclared one) is emitted as `(set (reg:SI 0 d0) (call ...))` (`calls.c:1604-1613, 395-406`), which makes d0 explicit, so the temporary is d1 as in the original; with a `void` declaration it is d0. Dumps: `m4p728-d1` (d0, `Spilling reg 0.`) and `m4p728-d2` (d1, `Spilling reg 1.`). The public GCC is a candidate, not the NeXT compiler; the proof is the byte comparison. The NeXTMach `ev_vars.h` declares `DoKbdEvent`, `process_device_mods` and `CalcModBit` as `extern void`; the first diagnostics left these out and the compiler warned 15 lines (implicit `int` call, then `void` definition) — with the nine declarations the object is the same and there is no warning (plan 728.5, user decision D164 ①).
