Changes from NeXTMach (all marked plan 759):
- Include lines (D104): `<sys/notify.h>` -> `<mach/notify.h>` (the 1997 `notification_t`), `<kern/ipc_copyin.h>` and `<kern/ipc_copyout.h>` removed (not in the 1997 headers), `#import <nextdev/snd_audio.h>` added (D170).
- Owners: `snd_var.sndinowner`, `sndoutowner` are queues `sndin_owners`, `sndout_owners` of `struct snd_owner` entries (`port`, `stream`, `link`; 16 bytes). New functions `snd_owner_add` (kalloc 16, `queue_enter`), `snd_get_owner` (external; finds the entry of a port; the form that gives the bytes was found after trying 16 loop forms), `snd_owner_remove` (`queue_remove`, `kfree`).
- Audio layer: new `snd_audio_not_loaded` (prints "SoundDSP: audio driver not loaded!" once through a function-local static and returns 5); the two device pointers (0) and twenty function pointers `__NXAudio*` (all `snd_audio_not_loaded`) are defined here in the order of the original `__data` [0x40b220c, 0x40b2268), the pointers are declared in `snd_audio.h`. Device parameters, buffers and streams of sound in and out go through these pointers; `snd_get_stream_formats`, `snd_nx_encoding` and `snd_set_stream_format` are new; `GET_SNDOUT_FORMATS` and `GET_SNDIN_FORMATS` (ids 115, 116), the `SND_MT_FORMAT` component (24 bytes) and the stream-format reply are new.
- `snd_port_alloc`: ports in `snd_var.task_space`. `snd_server_loop`: `snd_var.task_space`, `snd_var.task_map`, owner queues initialised, `ipc_port_copy_send(realhost.host_priv_self)`, only `snd_device_init(SND_DIR_PLAY)`, the `kernel_ipc_space`/`ipc_kernel` stores removed; the dispatch and the notification handling as NeXTMach.
- `snd_device_port_msg`: seventeen cases (jump table of 17 words, 68 bytes); sound out/in streams from the owner entries and `__NXAudioAddStream`/`PortToStream`; parameters through `GetSndoutOptions`/`SetSndoutOptions` (flags `_NXAUDIO_SNDOUT_*`); the owner cases add or abort through the queues; NEW_DEVICE_PORT loops over the owner queues without advancing the loop variable (the bytes need this form: the loop ends only when the queue is empty at its start, which is not what a source author would intend; it is the form that gives the bytes); RESET_SNDIN/SNDOUTOWNER replace the port of the entry.
- `snd_stream_port_msg`: device streams (bit 7 of the port) use the owner entry's stream and the audio layer (`StreamInfo`, `StreamControl`, `PlayStream`, `PlayStreamData`, `RecordStream`, `RecordStreamData`, `Get/SetBufferOptions`); dsp streams keep the NeXTMach region queues; a `SND_MT_FORMAT` component; `snd_link_abort()` before `snd_stream_abort()`; the abort/await/pause/resume requests per kind; `rate` of `PlayStream` is computed before the argument pushes.
- `snd_port_gone`: owner queues (the entry's stream is removed through `__NXAudioRemoveStream`), the message buffers freed only when allocated, the task map emptied only when no owner is left.
- Names that are this project's (D170): `snd_owner_add`, `snd_owner_remove`, `snd_get_stream_formats`, `snd_nx_encoding`, `snd_set_stream_format`, the field names of `struct snd_owner`, and the local names added in the marked regions (`o`, `oq`, `stream`, `is_dsp`, `owner_port`, `time_now`, `rate`, ...). The values of the `NX_SoundStream*`, `_NXAUDIO_*` and `SND_STREAM_FORMAT_*` constants (in `snd_audio.h`) are the ones that give the original bytes and agree with the Darwin 0.1 header values.
- Unchanged NeXTMach text: the notice, the static prototypes, the `snd_dbug` and `ASSERT` lines and comments outside the changed functions and where they do not change the object (restored by `gen_n3s.py`: comments, blank lines, `ASSERT` and `snd_dbug*` statements and the `#if DEBUG` block of `snd_server_loop`), the `thread_wakeup((int) &...)` and `kfree((caddr_t)...)` lines (four warnings, seven stderr lines with the three function-context lines; kept with `known_warnings`, as D121/D123/D126).
